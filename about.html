Our Commitment

Atithi Devo Bhava — The Marwari Tradition of Pure Hospitality

Situated right on Rajasthan State Highway 28 (RJ SH 28), Hotel Mahadev is a dedicated haven for families, business travelers, and tourist passengers traversing the Balotra, Sindhari, Sanchore, and Gujarat corridors.

Our foundational mission is to provide families, women, and children travelling long desert highways with a clean, dignified, and serene environment. Away from roadside dust and traffic noise, guests enjoy peaceful rest and authentic home-style food cooked in pure desi ghee.

100% Pure Vegetarian Kitchen

No eggs, meat, or alcohol are permitted on premises. Every dish is prepared fresh to order using pure spices and genuine desi ghee.

Private AC Family Dining Hall

Dedicated air-conditioned dining area specifically designed for families, women, and children to dine in comfort and dignity.

24/7 Generator Power Backup

Equipped with continuous heavy-duty generator backup to guarantee uninterrupted AC cooling and lighting throughout desert summers.

Expansive & Safe Parking Grounds

Multi-acre flat campus with ample turning radius for 50-seater tourist coaches, sleeper buses, trailers, and personal vehicles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Traveler & Guest FAQs

What are the check-in timings? Can guests check in late at night?

Hotel Mahadev provides 24 Hours / 7 Days round-the-clock check-in and check-out. Whether you arrive at midnight or 3:00 AM, our front desk and night staff are always on duty.

Is secure parking available for tourist coaches, trucks, and trailers?

Yes! Directly opposite Subhash College on RJ SH 28, we offer an expansive, flat, and secure parking campus accommodating 50-seater tourist buses, sleeper coaches, trailers, and personal cars under 24/7 security watch.

What is the dining policy for group travel and tourist bus passengers?

We have a 70+ capacity air-conditioned family dining hall. Tour operators and bus drivers can call +91-9509000904 approximately 30–45 minutes prior to arriving at Sindhari to pre-order fresh Dal Baati Churma, thalis, or custom meals.

Is the food 100% vegetarian and the atmosphere family-friendly?

Hotel Mahadev is strictly 100% pure vegetarian. No eggs, non-veg ingredients, or alcohol are allowed anywhere on premises. Dedicated AC dining space and clean western washrooms ensure complete comfort for families.
